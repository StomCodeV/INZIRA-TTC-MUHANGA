About the system

What is INZIRA?

INZIRA is a simple, paperless system for tracking student-intern movements at TTC Muhanga. It replaces the paper exit register with a fast, mobile-first tool that works on any phone — even without a dedicated computer at the gate.

📱

Built for the phone

Gatekeepers use their own phones. Big one-tap buttons, readable text, no training required.

⚡

Record in seconds

Tap “Check out” when an intern leaves, “Check in” when they return. Duration is computed automatically.

📊

See the full picture

School leadership sees who is out, which school they went to, and who is overdue — updated live.

How it works

Three steps. Every day.

The entire workflow is built around one simple cycle: leave, track, return.

1

Check out

The gatekeeper taps “Check out” on the student's card. The time is stamped instantly — no writing, no queues.

2

Live tracking

The admin dashboard shows every student currently out, which school they are at, and how long they have been gone.

3

Check in

When the student returns, the gatekeeper taps “Check in”. The system computes the duration and closes the record.

Key features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A small, focused set of features built around one problem: knowing where interns are, and when they come back.

🛰️

Real-time tracking

See who is out right now, updated the moment a gatekeeper taps.

🏫

Per-school breakdown

Every intern is grouped by their placement school — EP MBARE, GS SAINT ETIENNE, and more.

⏰

Overdue alerts

Anyone who has not returned after a set time is flagged in red — automatically.

📅

Daily reports

Per-day totals, per-school totals, and a missing-returns list, for any date range.

📤

CSV & print export

Export any report as CSV or print it — ready for the office, the district, or the Ministry.

🔒

Data stays on your device

No server, no login, no internet required. Records live inside the browser on the school's device.
