Why we built it

Why INZIRA

The paper book at the gate gets wet. It gets lost. At five o'clock, nobody knows who is still out. So we made INZIRA instead.

📱

Works on any phone

The gatekeeper does not need a computer. Any phone with a browser works. No installation. No training. No new equipment.

⚡

One tap is enough

Tap once when the student leaves. Tap again when they come back. The system does the rest.

📊

The office can see it

The office can check the list from any phone. No need to call the gate. No need to wait for a paper report.

It started with a pile of wet, half-filled books in the school office. Nobody could read last week's book. Nothing told us who was still out. So we built the tool we needed.

Day to day

How it works

Every day is the same. Students leave. You track them. They come back. That is all.

1

Students leave

The gatekeeper taps Check out. The time is saved. No pen. No queue.

2

You can see them

The dashboard shows every student who is out. It shows their school, and how long they have been gone.

3

Students come back

Tap Check in when they come back through the gate. The system counts the time.

Features

A short list

Every item on this list is something the gate or the office asked for. Nothing extra.

🛰️

Live list of who is out

Open the page. See every student who is out. It updates when the gate taps.

🏫

Grouped by school

Each student is filed under their practice school: EP MBARE, GS SAINT ETIENNE, EP KIGARAMA, and the rest.

⏰

Late returns are red

Anyone still out after six hours gets a red badge. You do not have to check the clock.

📅

Reports for the office

By day, by school, by session. Ready to print or send to the district.

📤

Export or print

Save a report as CSV. Or print it from the browser. No special software.

🔒

Records stay on the device

The gate does not need the internet. If the connection drops, work continues.

Two doors

Two doors. One register.

The gatekeeper and the administrator use the same records. Sign in and you go to the right page.
